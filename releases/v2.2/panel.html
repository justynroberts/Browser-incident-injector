      Setup

      1. In PagerDuty open Services → your service → Integrations.
      2. Add an Events API v2 integration and copy its 32-character key.
      3. Paste it under PagerDuty in this panel.
      4. Under Targets, list the button texts to intercept, e.g. checkout, sign in.
      5. Tick one or more scenarios, turn Injection on, then click a matching button.

      Nothing is intercepted until the match list has at least one entry. Ticked scenarios run together when a match fires, or straight away with Run. Stagger starts them a few seconds apart.
